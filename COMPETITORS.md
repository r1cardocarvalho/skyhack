# Itinerary competitors

Apps that sit next to Wanderlog, Tripsy, and TripIt. Grouped by how close they are to turning a booking into a live trip. Checked 26 Sep 2026 from vendor pages and 2026 roundups.

The AI-native TripIt pitch is already taken. Tineo’s homepage is “turns booking confirmations into itineraries,” with an assistant on top. TripWaffle already lists delays, jet lag, packing, and local safety. The open ground is the questions those timelines still do not answer: visa days, SIM and plugs, scams, and what is happening nearby.

## Same job: confirmation email becomes the itinerary

| Product | What it does | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TripIt](https://www.tripit.com/web/free) | The incumbent. Forward bookings to `plans@tripit.com`. Pro adds flight alerts and fare watch. | Free, Pro about $49/year |
| [Tineo](https://tineo.ai/) | The closest clone. Email import, day timeline, flight status, documents, group expenses, and an AI assistant. Web, iOS, and Android. | Free core, plus a subscription |
| [TripWaffle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tripwaffle/id6758685185) | Any provider, including ferries and small hotels. Delays, airport busyness, weather packing, jet lag, and local safety tips. Claims no paywall. | Free |
| [KAYAK Trips](https://www.kayak.com/) | Forward to `trips@kayak.com`. Flights, hotels, cars. Exists to drive more bookings. | Free |
| [Google Travel](https://www.google.com/travel) | Pulls reservations Google already sees in Gmail. A timeline, weak for sharing. | Free |
| [Tripsy](https://tripsy.app/) | Apple-first. Documents, expenses, calendar, collaboration. Email import is on Pro. | Free, Pro about $59/year or $299 once |

## Plan the days, then maybe import bookings

| Product | What it does | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/) | Maps, drag-and-drop days, group editing. Booking import and unlimited AI are Pro. | Free, Pro about $40/year |

| [Pilot](https://www.pilotplans.com/) | Itinerary, map, group editing, and in-app hotel booking. | Free |

## Generate a trip from a prompt

These do not organize an inbox.

| Product | What it does | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [Mindtrip](https://mindtrip.ai/) | Plans in chat and, since May 2026, books flights in chat via Sabre and PayPal. | Free |

## Gone

Sabre shut TripCase down on 1 April 2025 and deleted traveler data. That is why 2025–2026 reviews are full of people leaving for TripIt, KAYAK, and Tripsy. WorldMate and Google Trips are also gone. Google Trips folded into Google Travel.

## Where the idea list still has room

Matched against the ideas in the README and the public feature lists above.

| Idea | Already claimed by | Still open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flights and reservations from email | TripIt, KAYAK, Google, Tineo, TripWaffle, Tripsy Pro | Parsing itself is table stakes |
| Delays, gates, terminal | TripIt Pro, Flighty, Tineo, TripWaffle | Useful, not distinctive |
| Time zones and jet lag | TripWaffle | Thin coverage elsewhere |
| Chargers, packing | TripWaffle (weather packing), Stippl (packing list) | Plug types and adapters are rarely named |
| Visa and days in a country | Not in these feature lists | Schengen-style day counts are a clear gap |
| SIM cards | Not in these feature lists | Open |
| Events nearby and interests | Layla, Mindtrip, and Stippl plan activities before the trip | Live “what’s on while I’m here” is open |
| Common scams | TripWaffle says “safety info,” without naming scams | Open if it is specific to the city and the day |
| Ask “what’s next?” | Tineo has a planning assistant, not a live trip agent | The demo line if it runs on the itinerary you already imported |

Do not compete with Layla or Mindtrip on generating a holiday from a prompt, or with Wanderlog on a collaborative map. Both jobs are crowded and slow to show in ten hours. One forwarded confirmation, one timeline, then one answer that names the next flight, the plug, the visa days, and a scam to avoid is a demo the judges can see.

Prices are published list prices and can differ by store.
